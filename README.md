## 🧭 The BTC PRISM Approach

BTC uses the **PRISM** methodology to structure business transformation:

### P — Probe

Understand the business, its current performance and underlying challenges.

### R — Reframe

Convert problems into clearly defined improvement opportunities.

### I — Ignite

Develop practical initiatives that can create measurable business value.

### S — Steer

Guide implementation and keep improvement initiatives aligned with business priorities.

### M — Multiply

Measure results, sustain improvements and create a foundation for further growth.

The methodology follows a practical transformation cycle:

**Diagnose → Prioritize → Design → Implement → Measure & Sustain**.
